Bestie's daily loop

The question this answers: do the rules we agreed for the Bestie loop feel right once you live through a day? Those rules: Check-in → one Day plan with four Suggestions, swap and skip, Completion with Proof and a time gate, points → Levels → Status, Streak with a rest day, and quiet flags. Play freely on the left, or use the walkthroughs on the right to replay the awkward cases. The clock is simulated: use "+30 min" and "Next day". Nothing is saved.
